## Welcome to the most tremendous exoplanet transit animator 


[Planets in Motion](https://planetsinmotion.live) is an interactive
exoplanet transit simulator for exploring how planets passing in front
of their stars produce observable transit light curves.

## What it does

- Simulates exoplanet transits
- Visualizes planetary orbits and stellar transits
- Generates corresponding transit light curves
- Allows experimentation with orbital and planetary parameters
- Supports multiple planets and different orbital configurations

## Live Demo

https://planetsinmotion.live

## About

Planets in Motion is an educational tool for understanding exoplanet
transits, transit photometry, orbital inclination, transit depth, and
the relationship between an exoplanet's orbit and its observed light curve.
